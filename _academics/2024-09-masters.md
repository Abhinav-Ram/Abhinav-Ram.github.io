## Overview
To repeat my words from the main page, I enjoy exploring several methods to solve a particular project task while simultaneously paying respect to proven and effective ones in the literature. At the same time, I had a growing interest to step up from reiterating knowledge to create my own or applying the learned knowledge in highly novel and potentially unsolved problems. To me, doing a master's from a Scandinavian country, which are at the forefront when it comes to quality and relevancy of knowledge, felt like the right transition step to achieve my goals. On top of that, Uppsala University is the oldest university in Sweden and home to multiple Nobel Laureates and some of the smartest people in the world. ## Relevant Coursework
During my Bachelor's Program, I have been exposed to an Introduction in **Artificial Intelligence** and **Machine Learning**, along with **Data Science Foundations**. Building on that, I have taken up more advanced courses including **Statistical Machine Learning** and **Advanced Probabilistic Machine Learning**, and **Natural Computation Methods** such as **Neural Networks**, **Reinforcement Learning** and **Swarm Optimization methods**. My **Master's Thesis** also was at the intersection of clinical data and AI/ML systems. Of course, it wouldn't be an up-to-date education without **Large Language Models**.

## Projects
### Master's Thesis
I researched the development of a pipeline to transform heterogeneous EHR data into canonical knowledge-graph representations. A major part of my work involved profiling EHR tables, identifying suitable representations, comparing methodological approaches, and evaluating their performance on high-quality, de-identified, public medical datasets. I contributed to a unified, standard-agnostic framework for health data harmonization and knowledge graph construction.

### Course Projects
- Developed a scalable uncertainty-aware inference pipeline to obtain the posterior distribution of the diffusion tensor and baseline MRI signal for a single voxel, where I worked with real-world MRI data and compared multiple Bayesian inference methods: Metropolis-Hastings, Importance Sampling, Variational Inference, and the Laplace approximation. 
- Analysed the patient discharge process in COSMIC EHR to identify workflow inefficiencies and usability pain points through literature review and clinical survey, where I identified key issues including duplicate documentation and communication gaps, and proposed interface redesign using Nielsen’s usability heuristics.
- Worked on a SER project where I trained and evaluated three different models (Machine Learning, Deep Learning and Transfer Learning) for recognising speech emotion labels from an input Audio. Here I used the Interactive Emotional Dyadic Motion Capture (IEMOCAP) dataset, which consists of approximately 12 hours of audiovisual data.
- Designed and Implemented an LLM-powered Career Assistant with three action paths: General Q&A, Career Coach, and Cover Letter Generation. I implemented a TF‑IDF RAG system for context‑aware cover letter generation, integrated Google Calendar OAuth, designed a modular FastAPI back‑end and implemented cover letter quality evaluation using JD similarity (TF‑IDF cosine).
- Worked on a Social Data Mining Project where I analysed ∼1.7M tweets from the 2020 US Presidential Election to study structural and semantic polarisation across Trump and Biden discourse networks. E.g., I applied Louvain community detection to retweet and mention graphs, quantified semantic divergence using BERTopic and SharedLDA with Jensen‑Shannon divergence.
-  Implemented and benchmarked Sparse Matrix‑Vector Multiplication (SpMVM) for a Conjugate Gradient solver using CRS and SELL‑C‑σ formats on NVIDIA GPU via CUDA. I analysed format conversion and host‑device transfer overhead, proposing amortisation strategies and GPU‑side conversion to mitigate SELL‑C‑σ setup costs.
-  Developed a real‑time communication system between Two Raspberry Pi devices using MQTT Protocol and designed an intuitive User Interface to exchange messages.
